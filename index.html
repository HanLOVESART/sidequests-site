HOW IT WORKS

From group chat to booked, in one flow.

  1. 1

    Pick the vibe

    Dinner Night, Sports Day, Wellness Day and more. Set the time, area and budget, and choose your group.

  2. 2

    Vote on three picks

    The Classic, the Hidden Gem and the Why Not. Three good options, never three hundred. Everyone votes, the timer keeps it moving.

  3. 3

    We book it

    The winner gets booked inside the app, straight into the venue's own system. Everyone gets the confirmation.

  4. 4

    Split the bill

    The bill splits among the people who actually came. Pay your share with Apple Pay or Mada, and the night lands in your Quest Journal.
